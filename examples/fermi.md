## The Silent Sky

Look up on a clear night and you will see a few thousand stars. Each one is a
sun, and around a good fraction of them there should be planets. So where is
everybody?

## The Numbers

Our galaxy holds roughly two hundred billion stars. If even a tiny fraction
harbor life, the galaxy should be humming with civilisations. Yet the sky is
silent.

<!-- visual: slide | Two Hundred Billion Suns -->

## Fermi's Question

In 1950, over lunch, the physicist Enrico Fermi asked a simple question about
flying saucers and interstellar travel. The question became a legend: where is
everybody?

<!-- visual: slide | Where Is Everybody? -->

## Possible Answers

Maybe life is rare. Maybe civilisations destroy themselves. Maybe they are
already here and we simply cannot see them. Or maybe we are the first.
